Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

App Name: PlanMyPlate

Developer: Adam Corbin

This policy explains what PlanMyPlate collects, why, and who it is shared with. It applies to the PlanMyPlate apps for Android and iOS.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo. You can also add a phone number to your profile; this is optional.

Content you create. Your meals and recipes, weekly meal plans, grocery lists, and any meal photos you upload are stored on our servers so they sync across your devices and can be shared with people you choose.

Connections. When you connect with another person to share plans, we store the link between your two accounts. People you are connected with can see your name, email address, phone number (if you added one) and the plans you share with them.

Purchases. If you subscribe, we receive your subscription status and purchase history from Google Play or the App Store through RevenueCat. We never receive or store your card or payment details.

Usage and diagnostics. We collect which screens and features are used, an app-instance identifier, and crash reports. You can turn usage analytics off in the app under Settings → Privacy & Security.

Notification tokens. If you allow notifications, we store a device push token so we can send meal reminders and tell you when a partner changes a shared plan.

2. Device Permissions

  • Contacts. Used only so you can pick someone to share a plan with. Your contact list is read on your device and is not uploaded. We store only the email address of the person you choose.
  • Microphone. Used for voice entry of grocery items. Speech is transcribed by your device's speech recognition service (provided by Google on Android and Apple on iOS). We do not record, store or receive the audio.
  • Photos and camera. Used only when you choose to add a photo to a meal. Only the photo you select is uploaded.
  • Notifications. Used for meal reminders and shared-plan activity. You can turn these off in the app or in your device settings.

All of these are optional. The app works without them, and we do not collect your precise location.

3. AI Features and Recipe Import

Some features send text to our server, which forwards it to OpenAI for processing:

  • Generating a meal or a meal plan: the meal name or request you type.
  • Importing a recipe from a web link: the link you provide and the text of that web page.
  • Sorting grocery items into store sections: the item names.

We do not send your name, email address or other account details to OpenAI with these requests. Please do not enter personal information into these fields.

When you search for a restaurant, the search text is sent to Google Places. When a meal has no photo, we may look up a freely licensed photo of the dish by its name on Wikipedia.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sync your data across devices
  • To provide meal planning, grocery lists, sharing and the AI features described above
  • To manage subscriptions and restore purchases
  • To send the notifications you have enabled
  • To understand how the app is used, fix crashes and improve it
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information, and the app does not contain third-party advertising or track you across other apps and websites.

5. Service Providers

We share information with the following providers only as needed to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, database, file storage, push notifications, analytics and crash reporting
  • Google Sign-In: authentication
  • RevenueCat, Google Play Billing and Apple In-App Purchase: subscriptions
  • OpenAI: processing the AI requests described in section 3
  • Google Places: restaurant search

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Access to your data is restricted to your account and the people you choose to share with.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and its data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. This removes your profile, meals, plans, grocery lists, uploaded photos, connections and sign-in credential.
  • By request: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store. Purchase records held by Google, Apple and RevenueCat are kept under their own policies.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 years of age.
